Client

Global Jets LLC

Role

Web Designer & Developer

CASE STUDY

Who Is Global Jets

Global Jets LLC is an aviation holding company developing a portfolio of specialized subsidiaries to capture underserved opportunities across the aviation industry. I was brought on as a web developer and designer to build and manage the digital presence for companies operating under the Global Jets umbrella. Over the course of several focused two to three week sprints, I was responsible for creating two websites from the ground up and managing four existing ones.

01 / IDEATION & DESIGN

Ideation
& Design

Every project began in Figma, where I developed wireframes and visual concepts tailored to each brand's identity and target audience.

Private aviation clientele expect a certain level of sophistication, so the design direction across all sites prioritized clean layouts, strong typography, and a premium feel.

For Oneways.AI specifically, the design had to communicate both innovation and affordability, reflecting the company's unique model of using one way trips to offer clients access to private aviation at a lower price point.

Each design was built with the end user in mind before a single line of code was written.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Implementation
& Development

Development was handled entirely in WordPress, using Elementor and Divi to bring each design to life with precision and flexibility.

I built two sites from scratch, Oneways.AI and Instrumentrating.com, and managed four additional sites within the Global Jets portfolio including GlobalJet.com.

SEO web basics were implemented across all properties using All in One SEO, ensuring each site was optimized for search visibility from launch.

Each sprint ran two to three weeks, requiring clean project management and quick decision making to hit deadlines without sacrificing quality.

03 / FINAL RECAP

Final Recap

The completed sites gave Global Jets and its subsidiaries a polished, professional web presence across their entire portfolio. Oneways.AI launched as a clean, modern platform that clearly communicates its value proposition, offering clients a more accessible entry point into private aviation through one way charter pricing. GlobalJet.com and the remaining managed properties were brought up to standard with updated designs and improved SEO foundations.

It is worth noting that following the conclusion of my contract, the CEO brought on an additional development team and the sites have since been revised. The designs and builds delivered during my tenure represented the vision and requirements as defined at the time of each sprint.
